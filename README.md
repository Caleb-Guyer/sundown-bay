# Sundown Bay

Take the wheel in a coastal city with local jobs and police pursuits, or head offshore to race the outboard club.

[Play Sundown Bay](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/sundown-bay/)

Press **Play** to start on foot at the Marlin Motel. Amber **!** marks on the radar and city map lead to jobs. Walk up and press **F** to talk, or call a contact from your phone with **J**.

Use **WASD** or the arrow keys to move and steer. Move the mouse to look around. Press **E** beside a car to get in or out. **Space** is the handbrake while driving and jump on foot. **M** opens the map and **R** toggles sound.

For **Portside After Dark**, drive to Portside Tire and look for three cars under the work lights. Slow down beside Romy’s red coupe and honk with **H**. Follow the marked turns and cross the Cine Luna finish northbound within 90 seconds. Foundry Run and Mercado Loop alternate. A win pays **$260**, and Romy remembers the result. Drive on past and you can watch Jules take the challenge.

On foot, press **F** beside Jules’s teal coupe to ride shotgun. Use the mouse or arrows to frame Romy beneath the Cine Luna marquee. Press **Space** or click to take a photo, or **X** to recenter. Your best shot of the run becomes the next **Bay Wire** night cover. Reopen it with **J**.

For **One Good Take**, walk up to Romy beside the red No. 27 at Portside Tire and press **F**. Choose **Roll cameras**. As Jules, hold **W** to roll the flatbed. Keep **W** down and tap **G** on the green cue to switch to Romy and drive up the ramp. **A/D** steer, **S** brakes, and **G** can switch back to Jules in midair. All four wheels must land on the moving bed. Press **T** for a retake or wrap to see the actual still from your longest clean landing in the Cine Luna poster case. A clean landing unlocks a longer run-up.

Visit either pier and press **F** at the race desk to enter **Breakwater Run**. Pick the tight-turning **Needle 19** or the faster **Mako 22** and race Paz Ríos on three courses. Cross each gate between the red and green buoys in order. **W/S** work the throttle and reverse, **A/D** steer, and holding **Space** trims the bow up for faster straights and wider turns. First place earns **$220**, **$310** or **$400** depending on the course. Second place earns **$90**. Each course keeps a time record for each boat.

Choose **Free cruise** to explore the bay and Gull Light. You can also walk out to a moored boat and board with **E**. Follow the anchors on the **M** offshore chart to Marlin Pier or North Float. Slow below four knots alongside an open berth and press **E** to tie up and step ashore.

For **Last Ferry Out**, look for Inez Cruz and her red lunch tin outside the Marlin Motel. Pick her up in a car, park at the marked pier, and board a speedboat with **E**. Inez follows you aboard. Catch the **M/V Paloma**, steer alongside the amber gate on her left side, and hold a steady course for two seconds so Inez can jump. Hold **F** near the gate to match the ferry’s throttle. Standard sailing gives you 105 seconds at sea and pays **$450**. Five-star sailing gives you 85 seconds and pays **$900** for a transfer under Air One’s searchlight. Replays rotate through Gull Light Passage, North Channel and Outer Sound Crossing.

For **Last Exposure**, meet Mara Vega outside the Marlin Motel or call her through **J**. Pick her up and follow the street route to Port Sol. Park and press **F** to frame the Lot 14 notice. Click to focus or use the arrow keys, then press **Space** for the shutter. Deliver the photo to the Sol FM van in 55 seconds for **$425**, or take it to Cine Luna for **$300**. The evening edition of Bay Wire remembers which outlet ran your picture.

At three stars, patrol cars set up roadblocks. At four, some crews lay spike strips. Clip one and the car pulls toward the flat tire. At five, Air One joins the chase.

Board a boat at either pier while wanted and Harbor Patrol follows you offshore. One launch responds at one or two stars. At three stars a second launch moves to intercept. At five, the same Air One helicopter follows you over water with its searchlight. Hold **V** to look astern or open the offshore chart with **M** to see the patrol boats. If all units lose sight of you, they search your last reported position.

After you lose the cops, follow the wrench on the map to Portside Tire. Pull into the open bay and take your foot off the pedals. The shop replaces a flat for free.

Park in the same bay and press **F** for parts and paint. An **engine tune** costs **$250** and adds 18% engine power. **Sport tires** cost **$125** and add 25% cornering grip on pavement. Select **Respray** to preview six colors for **$75**. Fitted parts and paint stay on that individual car after you get out and are saved in the browser.

The game runs in the browser. To play locally, open `index.html` or serve this directory with `python -m http.server`. The overhead view is available when WebGL is unavailable.
