# Sundown Bay

Take the wheel in a coastal city with local jobs and police pursuits, or head offshore to race the outboard club.

[Play Sundown Bay](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/sundown-bay/)

Press **B** in your car to play your saved takes and cycle back to Sol FM. **R** still controls sound. Your cassettes are also on your desk in Room 14. After a good take you can ask June to go for a drive past her favorite places.

The yellow **Bay Cab** phone is on the beach side of Ocean Drive, a short walk south from the starter coupe. Press **F** at a phone, or flag down empty **Cab 17** from the curb. Pick a destination, then press **E** by the cab to ride with Mina Ortiz. Your first trip is free. In street view, press **F** to sit up front and move the mouse to look around. Press **E** during the ride to ask for the next curb. The four ranks are marked on the city map.

Just south of the Marlin, take a **beach cruiser** from the bicycle rack with **E**. **W** pedals, **Shift** sprints, **S** brakes, **Space** hops and **H** rings the bell. Stop and press **E** to park. Each cruiser stays where you left it between visits. The phone can locate the nearest one.

At **Sol Beach Courts** or **Mercado Yard**, walk up to the score table and press **F**. Shoot around with a local rebounder, or call next for a game to five. **X** squares you to the basket. Hold **Space** and let go in the green band. **G** switches between the rim and the backboard. On offense, **F** crosses over. On defense, **F** reaches for a steal and **Space** jumps for a block or rebound. Clear the arc after a defensive rebound or steal. Shots from beyond it count for two. Press **E** to step off.

Dee pays **$85** for a win at the beach. Inés pays **$135** behind El Mercado. A first win at either court also puts its **No. 14 jersey** on the clothes rail in your motel room. Both scorekeepers remember your record and longest make. Locals play when you drive by. Pull onto the court and they step aside, then check the ball back in after you move the car.

For **Last Bell Run**, speak to Javi Reyes on the promenade or Mags Duran at the **Coast Wheel** ticket window. Ride alongside Javi in the same direction and **hold F** to match his speed and take the red pouch. Brake and step off at the window, then press **F** to hand Mags the roll. The job pays **$115**, with another **$40** for beating the bell. Miri and Len take their tickets up the ramp together.

Walk up the wooden ramp and press **E** at the Coast Wheel turnstile. The first ride and rides earned on ticket runs are free. Another lap costs **$8**. Move the mouse or use **Q/C** to look across the bay, and press **V** to switch between the cabin and an outside view. Mags lets you off at the platform after one full turn.

To earn a fare yourself, stop near a waving local in your own car and press **H**. Walt Dunn is carrying a film tin, Bea Salas a bouquet, and Otis Reed a snare. Leave room at the passenger door when you drop them off. A smooth ride earns up to **$30** extra. Mina also picks up the regulars while you’re doing other things.

The box office at **Cine Luna** is beside its cab stand on Neon Mile. Press **F** at the doors, walk to the program case or an aisle seat and press **F** again. *The Last Pelican* plays on a loop. Once Walt brings his tin, *After the Last Tram* joins the program. Look back at the booth after he walks in. Land a jump in *One Good Take* to put your best still on the screen. Deliver Mara’s Lot 14 photograph to the theater to add her print to the pre-show.

Follow **Ocean Drive** north past the Boardwalk to **Pelican Cut**. The paved causeway and the shell levee meet again at the fish camp. Ada watches the spoonbills from the hide. Nell carries ice down to the east pier, where you can board a skiff and return to town by sea. The **Pelican Cut** tab on the map shows both roads and the landing.

At **Salt Creek Drawbridge**, traffic stops while the supply boat *June Bug* runs ice up the creek and takes the empty totes back to the bay. Follow Shell Levee at the fork to drive around an opening. From a skiff, follow the channel marks and press **F** near the fenders to call for passage. At the head of the creek, slow at the ladder and press **E** to tie up. Leda Vale unloads the ice at the pier, and the boardwalk leads back to Shell Levee.

Meet **Inez Soria** on the keeper’s platform on the south bank to take **The Salt Creek Watch**. Press **F** when the boat calls, wait for the road to clear, and hold **Space** to raise the leaves. Release the lever as the vessel passes. Once the stern clears, hold **Space** to lower, then press **F** to open the gates. Hold **V** for a view down the channel. A signed watch pays **$95–$125** and stays in Inez’s log.

Walk past the striped **Sol Beach lifeguard tower** across from the Marlin and head straight into the surf. Hold **Space** to dive, release it to rise, and hold **Shift** for a faster stroke. Press **E** at the east ladder of any public pier or beside a slow skiff to climb aboard. Offshore, slow the skiff below four knots and press **E** to go over the side.

When a swimmer waves beyond the flags, Tali Moreno leaves the tower to bring them in. You can swim alongside, press **F** to take the float, and tow them into shallow water for **$95**. Press **F** on foot at the tower’s lost-and-found board for clues to three objects on the bay floor. Recover one with **F** and bring it back for the owner’s reward. Filed objects hang on the tower and stay there between visits.

For **The Marsh Line**, find Kit Bell at the camp’s Sol FM field station and press **F** on foot. Pick the spoonbill flock, the wind pump, or the shrimp boat *Little Dot*. At the listening spot, press **F** to raise the recorder. Aim with the mouse or arrows, then hold **Space** for 2.6 seconds. Bring a take back to Kit to earn money. Sol FM plays your recording on your next ride, and the phone keeps your best tape for each subject. The visual meter and captions work with sound off.

Park a car in the space painted **14**, press **F**, and put the Marlin guest permit on its dash. When you step out of that car, the map remembers where you parked it, including after a browser restart. Open the phone or city map to locate it. A permit can move to another car in space 14. The street atlas and driving directions share the same street names.

**An Extra Plate** starts at Rosario’s night counter on the south frontage of **El Mercado**, serving **6 PM–2 AM**. Walk up, press **F**, and choose one of two orders. Hot food earns a tip for quick delivery, and the thermos and flan travel best with a smooth ride. Park at the customer’s workplace, step out and press **F** to hand over the bag. Rosario rotates the slips, the regulars remember you, and their receipts accumulate on the motel desk.

For **Portside After Dark**, drive to Portside Tire and look for three cars under the work lights. Slow down beside Romy’s red coupe and honk with **H**. Follow the marked turns and cross the Cine Luna finish northbound within 90 seconds. Foundry Run and Mercado Loop alternate. A win pays **$260**, and Romy remembers the result. Drive on past and you can watch Jules take the challenge.

On foot, press **F** beside Jules’s teal coupe to ride shotgun. Use the mouse or arrows to frame Romy beneath the Cine Luna marquee. Press **Space** or click to take a photo, or **X** to recenter. Your best shot of the run becomes the next **Bay Wire** night cover. Reopen it with **J**.

For **One Good Take**, walk up to Romy beside the red No. 27 at Portside Tire and press **F**. Choose **Roll cameras**. As Jules, hold **W** to roll the flatbed. Keep **W** down and tap **G** on the green cue to switch to Romy and drive up the ramp. **A/D** steer, **S** brakes, and **G** can switch back to Jules in midair. All four wheels must land on the moving bed. Press **T** for a retake or wrap to see the actual still from your longest clean landing in the Cine Luna poster case. A clean landing unlocks a longer run-up.

Visit either pier and press **F** at the race desk to enter **Breakwater Run**. Pick the tight-turning **Needle 19** or the faster **Mako 22** and race Paz Ríos on three courses. Cross each gate between the red and green buoys in order. **W/S** work the throttle and reverse, **A/D** steer, and holding **Space** trims the bow up for faster straights and wider turns. First place earns **$220**, **$310** or **$400** depending on the course. Second place earns **$90**. Each course keeps a time record for each boat.

Choose **Free cruise** to explore the bay and Gull Light. You can also walk out to a moored boat and board with **E**. Follow the anchors on the **M** offshore chart to Marlin Pier or North Float. Slow below four knots alongside an open berth and press **E** to tie up and step ashore.

For **Last Ferry Out**, look for Inez Cruz and her red lunch tin outside the Marlin Motel. Pick her up in a car, park at the marked pier, and board a speedboat with **E**. Inez follows you aboard. Catch the **M/V Paloma**, steer alongside the amber gate on her left side, and hold a steady course for two seconds so Inez can jump. Hold **F** near the gate to match the ferry’s throttle. Standard sailing gives you 105 seconds at sea and pays **$450**. Five-star sailing gives you 85 seconds and pays **$900** for a transfer under Air One’s searchlight. Replays rotate through Gull Light Passage, North Channel and Outer Sound Crossing.

For **Last Exposure**, meet Mara Vega outside the Marlin Motel or call her through **J**. Pick her up and follow the street route to Port Sol. Park and press **F** to frame the Lot 14 notice. Click to focus or use the arrow keys, then press **Space** for the shutter. Deliver the photo to the Sol FM van in 55 seconds for **$425**, or take it to Cine Luna for **$300**. The evening edition of Bay Wire remembers which outlet ran your picture.

At three stars, patrol cars set up roadblocks. At four, some crews lay spike strips. Clip one and the car pulls toward the flat tire. At five, Air One joins the chase.

Board a boat at either pier while wanted and Harbor Patrol follows you offshore. One launch responds at one or two stars. At three stars a second launch moves to intercept. At five, the same Air One helicopter follows you over water with its searchlight. Hold **V** to look astern or open the offshore chart with **M** to see the patrol boats. If all units lose sight of you, they search your last reported position.

After you lose the cops, follow the wrench on the map to Portside Tire. Pull into the open bay and take your foot off the pedals. The shop replaces a flat for free.

Hard impacts crease individual panels, crack glass and can leave the radiator steaming. A damaged front end costs acceleration. A badly hit traffic car will look for space to pull over, flash its hazards and let its driver out. Portside quotes bodywork by the damage on that car.

Ordinary street traffic and parked cars carry their weight in a collision. Catch a back corner and both cars twist. Hit a hatch squarely and it rolls farther than a panelvan. The suspension kicks, sliding tires leave paired tracks, and wet pavement gives them more room to run. A rattled driver brakes, then works back toward their lane once there is space.

For **Portside Recovery**, walk up to **Della Moss** beside the green wrecker at Portside Tire, or talk to a stranded driver. Take Iona, Rafa or Liv’s call, get into the wrecker with **E** and stop alongside to let them board. Face the same way as their car, stop and press **G** to extend the wheel lift. Hold **V** for a close view of the crossbar, reverse it under the front tires and press **G** again to lift. Tow the car into the striped lane at Portside and press **G** at a stop to set it down. Della works on it, the driver takes the keys and the radio logs your pay. A smooth recovery earns up to **$30** extra.

You can take the wrecker between calls to tow your own car. Della repairs the bodywork and a flat free of charge when you bring it in on the lift. **J → Della Moss** marks the workbench.

Park in the same bay and press **F** for parts and paint. An **engine tune** costs **$250** and adds 18% engine power. **Sport tires** cost **$125** and add 25% cornering grip on pavement. Select **Respray** to preview six colors for **$75**. Fitted parts and paint stay on that individual car after you get out and are saved in the browser.

The game runs in the browser. To play locally, open `index.html` or serve this directory with `python -m http.server`. The overhead view is available when WebGL is unavailable. The game loads its JavaScript in one request. After editing the individual scripts, run `python build_bundle.py` and commit the updated `index.html` and `sundown.js`. The ordered source list lives in `index.html`.
