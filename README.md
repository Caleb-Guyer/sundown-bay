# Sundown Bay

Take the wheel in a coastal city with local jobs, police pursuits and a playable story.

[Play Sundown Bay](https://caleb-guyer.github.io/sundown-bay/)

Use **WASD** or the arrow keys to move and steer. Move the mouse to look around. Press **E** beside a car to get in or out. **Space** is the handbrake while driving and jump on foot. **M** opens the map, **J** opens jobs, and **R** toggles sound.

Choose **Play Last Exposure** on the title screen or call Mara Vega through **J**. Pick her up outside the Marlin Motel and follow the street route to Port Sol. Park and press **F** to frame the Lot 14 notice. Click to focus or use the arrow keys, then press **Space** for the shutter. Deliver the photo to the Sol FM van in 55 seconds for **$425**, or take it to Cine Luna for **$300**. The evening edition of Bay Wire remembers which outlet ran your picture.

At three stars, patrol cars set up roadblocks. At four, some crews lay spike strips. Clip one and the car pulls toward the flat tire. At five, Air One joins the chase.

After you lose the cops, follow the wrench on the map to Portside Tire. Pull into the open bay and take your foot off the pedals. The shop replaces a flat for free.

Park in the same bay and press **F** for parts and paint. An **engine tune** costs **$250** and adds 18% engine power. **Sport tires** cost **$125** and add 25% cornering grip on pavement. Select **Respray** to preview six colors for **$75**. Fitted parts and paint stay on that individual car after you get out and are saved in the browser.

The game runs in the browser. To play locally, open `index.html` or serve this directory with `python -m http.server`. The overhead view is available when WebGL is unavailable.
